Checking the SMS consent box is optional. Leave it unchecked and we'll only follow up by email or phone — no text messages. You can also opt in or out of SMS later by texting START or STOP to 312-815-4001.

What you'll receive

Only operational messages — never marketing or promotions. Examples:

  • Maintenance ticket created / scheduled / resolved
  • Contractor heading to your unit, with an ETA
  • Lease renewal reminder when your lease enters its renewal window
  • Rent reminder before each month's due date
  • Replies to questions you've texted us

SMS terms at a glance

Program
Poblano Rentals operational SMS — for current tenants, prospective tenants, owners, and contractors.
Message frequency
Varies based on activity at your unit or building. Most users receive fewer than 5 messages per month.
Carrier fees
Message and data rates may apply. Your wireless carrier — not Poblano Rentals — sets these rates.
Opt out
Reply STOP to any of our messages to immediately unsubscribe. You'll receive one confirmation message and then no further texts unless you opt back in. UNSUBSCRIBE, END, QUIT, and CANCEL also work.
Help
Reply HELP to any of our messages to receive a brief reply explaining who we are and how to reach us.
Mobile information sharing
Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All consent and the underlying mobile information remains with Poblano Rentals.
Supported carriers
All major US carriers, including AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile, US Cellular, and Sprint.
