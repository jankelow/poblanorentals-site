Text-message communication

Poblano Rentals uses SMS to communicate with property owners, tenants, prospective tenants, and contractors who have explicitly opted in.

Messages are operational only — maintenance ticket updates, contractor scheduling, lease renewal reminders, rent reminders, and replies to inbound questions. No marketing or promotions.

To opt in: text START to 312-815-4001, or use the form on our SMS opt-in page. You can reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for help. Message frequency varies (most users receive fewer than 5 messages per month). Message and data rates may apply.

Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All SMS opt-in data and consent is held by Poblano Rentals only. See Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for full details.
